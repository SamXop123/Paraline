# The Multi-Monitor Studio Update

Paraline 2.7.0 transforms your multi-display setup into a unified audio-visual powerhouse. This major release introduces the **Multi-Monitor Studio Suite**—featuring Desk Symmetry for dual and triple monitors, Seamless Panoramic Spanning across horizontal and stacked screens, smart bezel seam suppression, and an engine overhaul that optimizes every theme for multi-screen workspaces.

---

## 🔥 The Multi-Monitor Studio Suite

### 1. Desk Symmetry (Dual & Triple Screen Mirroring)
Turn multiple screens into a perfectly balanced desk battle station:
- **Dual Monitors**: Edge visualizers mirror outward (left monitor frames the left side of your desk, right monitor frames the right side), framing your workstation with bilateral symmetry.
- **Triple Monitors**: Outer monitors mirror outward while your center screen serves as a balanced focal point, keeping visual noise away from your primary workspace or game viewport.

### 2. Panoramic Canvas Span (Horizontal & Vertically Stacked)
Link multiple monitors into one massive, continuous soundstage:
- **Horizontal & Stacked Display Support**: Visualizers now flow seamlessly across side-by-side screens or vertically stacked (top & bottom) monitor setups.
- **Smart Bezel Seam Suppression**: Automatically hides interior borders between touching monitors, eliminating distracting double-thick lines where your bezels meet.

### 3. Multi-Screen Theme Overhaul
Every theme has been rebuilt and tuned for multi-display environments:
- **Side Bars as Desk Bookends**: The left bar now stays on your leftmost monitor and the right bar stays on your rightmost monitor, keeping middle screens completely clear.
- **Wave Continuity (Ambient Wave, Flow Border, Ripple Flow)**: Audio waves and perimeter borders now transition smoothly across screen boundaries with zero stutter or phase disruption.
- **Particles & Horizon (Dot Particles, Snow, Synthwave Horizon)**: Full virtual coordinate rendering ensures particles drift across screen borders without getting clipped at monitor seams.
- **No More Lost Visualizers**: Fixed an issue where certain themes could disappear or fail to render on primary displays when changing modes.

### 4. Flexible Display Targeting & Split Themes
- **Display Selector**: Choose whether Paraline runs across all connected displays, strictly on your primary screen, or pinned to a secondary monitor.
- **Per-Display Customization**: Pair different themes and colorways with different monitors to customize each display’s vibe.

### 5. Dynamic Hotplugging & Tray Awareness
- **Auto-Recovery on Screen Changes**: Plugging in a monitor, unplugging a laptop from a dock, or changing Windows display scaling automatically recalibrates the visualizer canvas with zero restarts needed.
- **Multi-Screen Tray Menu**: System tray and overlay menus now open right where your cursor is on whichever display you are using.

## ✨ More Updates in Paraline 2.7

- **Dual Theme Mode is Now Stable**: Our Day & Night automatic theme scheduler has graduated out of beta and is now a core feature.
- **In-App Contact & Feedback**: Added a clean, built-in feedback window directly in Settings and the right-click menu, making it easy to report bugs or share feature ideas with me anytime.
- **Performance & Canvas Scaling**: Reduced GPU overhead and perfected high-DPI scaling across mixed refresh rate monitors (e.g. 144Hz display + 60Hz display).
